FVN.li mark variants

Round 3: five treatments of the spine. The page halves and face are the current mark; only the centre changes. Shown at 180px, 32px and 16px in the dark-theme and light-theme accent colours.

Current

The mark in use today: an open gap with a small nose.

A · Closed gutter

The halves move together until only a hairline fold remains, and the nose is dropped. Reads as one folded sheet rather than two separate wings.

B · Spine bar

The halves move apart and a solid bar runs the full height between them, ending in a point where the nose was.

C · Spine cap

The gap stays open and the nose is replaced by a wider chevron underneath, like the binding seen from the bottom edge holding both pages.

D · Wedge gutter

The gap becomes a narrow V that closes at the bottom, so the pages hinge from a bound point instead of floating apart. No nose.

E · Bound base

The nose grows upward into a solid block that fuses the lower part of the halves; only the top stays open, like pages fanning from a spine.
