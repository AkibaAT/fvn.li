# Rater Commands Overview

Rater commands handle the management of user rating behavior and moderation of suspicious rating activity.

## Commands

| Command                 | Purpose                              | Key Options                 |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| `rater:mark-suspicious` | Mark or unmark a rater as suspicious | `--reason=TEXT`, `--unmark` |

Rater commands focus on content moderation and quality control for the rating system, helping maintain the integrity of
user-generated ratings and reviews.
